Ometria provides a customer data and experience platform (CDXP) tailored for retail brands, leveraging AI to deliver personalized, cross‑channel marketing and commerce experiences. The platform unifies data ingestion, identity resolution, segmentation, predictive insights, and orchestration across email, SMS, push, web, in‑store and more, helping retailers increase repeat rates, revenue and efficiency.

**Areas:** Company · Retail · Customer Data Platform · AI · Marketing

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Ometria: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
